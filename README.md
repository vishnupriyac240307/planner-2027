# 2027 Digital Planner | Premium Productivity Dashboard

An elegant, modern, personalized **2027 Digital Planner & Life Operating System** designed specifically for high-achieving professionals and students using macOS.

Inspired by a seamless blend of **Notion**, **Google Calendar**, and **digital journal aesthetics**, featuring a blue-themed palette with soft pastel blues, deep navy, subtle lavender accents, rounded cards, and smooth micro-interactions.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Sections

### 1. Main Dashboard
- **Welcome Message**: Personalized greeting with daily focus mantra: *"Welcome to your 2027"*.
- **Live Digital Clock**: Real-time 12-hour digital clock with seconds and date tracking.
- **2027 Year Progress Indicator**: Real-time progress bar tracking day $X$ of $365$ ($0\% - 100\%$).
- **Monthly Progress Bar**: Tracking elapsed percentage of the active month.
- **Today's Priorities & Focus**: Top 3 North Star daily goals and editable focus banner.
- **Daily Habits Completion Widget**: Live percentage ring and quick one-click check-ins.
- **Upcoming Events & Holidays**: Next 3 schedule items and upcoming Indian festivals.
- **Motivational Quote of the Day**: Rotating philosophical and productivity quotes.
- **Quick-Access Navigation**: 1-click jump to any planner section.

### 2. 2027 Calendar (Jan 1, 2027 – Dec 31, 2027)
- **4 Rich View Modes**:
  - **Monthly Calendar**: Full interactive grid with clickable days, task count badges, event markers, and holiday highlights.
  - **Yearly Overview**: All 12 months at a glance with quick date jumping.
  - **Weekly Planner**: 7-day spread with day-by-day appointments.
  - **Daily Hourly Timeline**: Interactive hourly timeline from **5:00 AM to 11:00 PM**.
- **Accurate 2027 Dates**: Verified non-leap year (365 days), January 1, 2027 starts on Friday.
- **Event Scheduling Modal**: Add, edit, and delete events with custom color accents, locations, and time slots.
- **Weekend Accents & Date Highlighting**: Distinctive styling for Saturdays and Sundays.

### 3. Daily Planner
- **Top 3 Priorities**: Visual non-negotiable focus anchors for each day.
- **Hourly Schedule**: Granular time-blocking from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
- **Interactive To-Do Checklist**: Fast inline task creation, priority tags, and completion toggles.
- **Dedicated Sub-Planners**:
  - Study / Academics plan
  - Work & Engineering project tasks
  - Personal activities & errands
- **Daily Reflection & Mindfulness**:
  - *"What went well today?"*
  - *"What can I improve tomorrow?"*
  - **Hydration Tracker**: 8-glass visual tracker.
  - **Mood & Energy Rating**.
  - General Scratchpad / Freeform Notes.

### 4. Monthly Planner
- Monthly view for all 12 months of 2027.
- Monthly anchor focus theme and high-impact goals.
- Monthly action checklist.
- Important dates and Indian festivals for that specific month.
- Financial income, expense, and savings snapshot.
- Monthly achievements & proud wins log.
- End-of-month review and adjustments.

### 5. Yearly Goals & 2027 Vision Board
- **Vision Board**: Aesthetic Pinterest/Notion-style photo cards with captions and inspiring quotes.
- **Categorized Goals**: Academic, Career, Financial, Health & Fitness, Skills to Learn, Books to Read, Projects to Complete, Places to Visit, Experiences, Personal.
- **Interactive Progress Sliders**: Drag to adjust goal completion percentage ($0\% - 100\%$).
- **Status & Priority Badges**: Deadlines, targets, and notes.

### 6. Student & Academic Planner
- **Subject-Wise Syllabus Progression**: Custom subject colors, course codes, and instructor details.
- **Exam Countdown Timetable**: Days-remaining counter with urgent alerts for upcoming exams.
- **Assignment & Lab Tracker**: Status tracking (*Pending*, *In Progress*, *Submitted*, *Graded*).
- **Spaced Revision Planner**: Scheduled topic review checklist.
- **Study Session Logger**: Log focus sessions, durations, topics, and focus ratings (1–5 ⭐).
- **Academic Hours Chart**: Visual bar chart breakdown of study time per course (powered by Recharts).

### 7. Habit Tracker (All 12 Months)
- **31-Day Habit Grid**: Interactive matrix for every day of the selected month.
- **Streak Calculation**: Real-time current streak tracking with fire badges (🔥).
- **Sample Pre-Configured Habits**: Study & Deep Learning, Read 20+ Pages, Exercise, Drink 3L Water, Sleep by 11:00 PM, Practice Coding, Journaling (all editable and removable).
- **Completion Analytics**: Recharts visual bar chart of completion rates per habit.

### 8. Personal Finance Planner (₹ INR Default)
- **Monthly Inflows, Outflows & Savings**: Net savings rate percentage.
- **Category Spending Breakdown**: Interactive Recharts Pie Chart.
- **Budget Category Caps**: Visual threshold bars with amber/red budget alert indicators.
- **Sinking Funds & Financial Goals**: Progress towards emergency reserves, MacBook upgrade, travel funds.
- **Transaction Ledger**: Complete table with payment method tags (UPI, Credit Card, Net Banking).

### 9. Digital Journal & Notes
- **Mood Tracking**: 6 mood states with custom emoji tags (*Ecstatic*, *Happy*, *Neutral*, *Tired*, *Stressed*, *Sad*).
- **Gratitude Journal**: 3 daily gratitude prompts.
- **Brain Dump Canvas**: Unload mental chatter and unstructured thoughts.
- **Favorite Memories**: Star bookmarking system to review highlight moments.
- **Instant Search & Tag Filtering**: Search across entries, dates, tags, and gratitude lists.

### 10. Important Dates & Indian Festivals 2027
- **Pre-Populated 2027 Indian Gazetted Holidays & Festivals**:
  - Republic Day (Jan 26), Makar Sankranti / Pongal (Jan 14), Maha Shivratri (Feb 12), Holi (Mar 22–23), Good Friday (Mar 26), Eid-ul-Fitr (Apr 9–10), Ambedkar Jayanti (Apr 14), Independence Day (Aug 15), Raksha Bandhan (Aug 24), Ganesh Chaturthi (Sep 4), Gandhi Jayanti (Oct 2), Dussehra (Oct 19), Diwali / Lakshmi Puja (Nov 7–8), Guru Nanak Jayanti (Nov 23), Christmas (Dec 25), etc.
- **Regional / Lunar Calendar Variation Indicators**: Clear markers noting that lunar sightings and state gazettes may vary by 1 day.
- **Countdowns**: Live calculation of days until each occasion.

### 11. Preferences & Data Portability
- **Theme Modes**: Light Mode, Dark Navy Mode, and System Theme.
- **Backup Export**: 1-click JSON download (`planner-2027-backup.json`).
- **Backup Restore**: Upload JSON file to restore entire state.
- **Safe Reset**: Factory reset confirmation modal to revert to pristine sample template.

### 12. Progressive Web App (PWA) & Native Mac App Experience
- **Custom Blue App Icon**: High-definition macOS squircle app icon (SVG & PNG up to 512x512) with a royal blue/navy gradient, calendar binder rings, "2027" emblem, and golden sparkle accent.
- **Standalone Full-Screen Window**: Opens without browser navigation bars, tabs, or bookmarks clutter (`display: standalone`).
- **Complete Offline Support**: Service Worker (`sw.js`) caches the full app shell, icons, fonts, and scripts so you can plan, view, and organize your year without an internet connection (e.g. on flights).
- **1-Click Installation**: In-app "Install App" button in the header and settings page with browser-specific instructions for Safari (**File > Add to Dock...**) and Chrome (**Install App** icon).
- **Dock & Spotlight Integration**: Launchable directly from your macOS Dock or via `⌘ + Space` Spotlight search.

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
- **`N`**: Quick Add New Task modal (anywhere on the dashboard).
- **`⌘ + K` / `Ctrl + K`**: Open Global Search modal.
- **`Esc`**: Dismiss any open modal dialog.
